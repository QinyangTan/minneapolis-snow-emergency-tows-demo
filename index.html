01 / THE RULES

Same storm.
Different streets.

Each emergency day has a distinct restriction. Those differences are central to this analysis.

01 DAY 1 · FROM 9 PM

Snow Emergency Routes

No parking on either side of Snow Emergency routes (until fully plowed).

02 DAY 2 · FROM 8 AM

Even sides + parkways

No parking on even sides of non-emergency-route streets or either side of parkways (until fully plowed).

03 DAY 3 · FROM 8 AM

Odd sides

No parking on odd sides of non-emergency-route streets (until fully plowed).

Simplified rule summaries. Streets may reopen after they are fully plowed. Read the current official City parking rules ↗

09 / METHODS & CAVEATS

What the data
can — and can't — say.

Source

City of Minneapolis official ArcGIS Snow Emergency tow FeatureServer layers and neighborhood boundaries.

Interpretation

Counts represent observed enforcement actions; they do not measure illegal parking prevalence or individual towing probability.

Location uncertainty

Some City tow-layer metadata cites an expected address geocoding error rate of approximately 8–14%. We prioritize neighborhood-level aggregation.

Complete emergencies

The selected nine events include all three days. The available Victory 2 dataset is excluded because it only contains Day 1 records.
